# InstaBazaar Design System

## Brand Identity

InstaBazaar is a modern Social Commerce Catalog Management Platform designed for Instagram businesses. The interface should feel premium, minimal, friendly, and startup-like rather than corporate or overly colorful.

---

## Design Principles

* Minimal
* Modern SaaS
* Spacious Layout
* Rounded Components
* Clean Typography
* Instagram Inspired
* Off-White Background
* Soft Shadows
* Professional
